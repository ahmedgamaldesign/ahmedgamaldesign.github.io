AI design systems

Three systems I built so a small team can produce like a big one.

Three design systems I built in Claude, each a team of AI agents trained on my own design rules, direction and notes. They design with almost zero mistakes: no spelling errors, wrong logos or wrong brand elements. I set the standard and review the output.

01

Gulf Social Studio

From a brief and a brand name to finished, on-brand posts in four directions.

Launching
The problem

Gulf brands need a steady stream of on-brand posts in Arabic and English. General AI tools get Arabic type, logos and regional details wrong.

You give it

A brief or a campaign file + the brand name

→
It returns

Four finished directions for every post; in a campaign, four full series

How it works · agents and my checkpoints
  1. Read the brief
  2. Study the brand’s own feed
  3. Plan four directions
  4. Set the type
  5. Design
  6. Check every detail and fix it
  7. Review pageMy review
  • Every job returns four directions: Continue (the brand’s own social look, which the studio finds on its feed), Proven (what works in its field), Fresh (a style borrowed from a neighbouring field) and a fourth option. In a campaign, each direction is a full series, so the posts belong together.
  • Built on a research library of 292 Saudi and Gulf brand accounts across eight fields (benchmark brands, research and business, medical, academic, technology, real estate, retail and AI creators): 3,081 posts coded one by one, turned into 138 tested styles and 65 house rules. No AI model is trained on a Gulf-only library at this density: the difference isn’t the model, it’s what the system gives it.
  • Type is set first, in real Arabic and Latin typefaces chosen for the job; logos keep their true proportions; every letter, figure and logo is checked before anything reaches me. Trained on my direction and review of every detail: Arabic typography, real products, regional dress and Gulf settings.

02

Gulf Corporate Deck System

Learns a style from one or two decks you like, then builds a new deck in it.

Used on client work
The problem

Gulf corporate and government clients want decks that feel familiar, in a style they already like, without copying an old file.

You give it

Your content + one or two reference decks

→
It returns

A new corporate deck in a similar style, never a copy

How it works · agents and my checkpoints
  1. Analyse the reference style
  2. Plan the deck
  3. Design
  4. Make photos and icons
  5. Check every detail and fix it
  6. Review pageMy review
  • Reads the reference decks in full (colours, type, layouts, image language) and turns them into a style it can design with.
  • A design agent works inside the design logic I wrote: how each kind of content becomes a layout, and how far it may go creatively while staying true to the style it learned.
  • An image agent researches the client’s real products, places and logos and puts them into the AI photos; an icon agent draws icons around the brand’s own logo instead of generic sets. A review agent and a research-and-fix agent check every slide, so nothing is repeated, wasted or wrong.
  • Made for Gulf corporate and government-style decks, where the look has to feel familiar to the client without copying an old file.

03

Muhimma Deck System

A raw proposal in, a finished deck in Muhimma’s style out.

In daily use
The problem

Muhimma’s CEO sends proposals and reports that must look designed, in the house style, often within hours.

You give it

A raw proposal: text, a Word file or rough slides

→
It returns

A finished PowerPoint in Muhimma’s house style

How it works · agents and my checkpoints
  1. Read the content
  2. Research the brand
  3. Design every slide
  4. Make photos and icons
  5. Check every detail and fix it
  6. Review pageMy review
  • Muhimma is a Riyadh market research company; I have designed its CEO’s proposals and its reports as a consultant since September 2025. I built this system for that work.
  • It runs on the same engine as the Gulf Corporate Deck System: the same design, image, icon, review and research-and-fix agents, set to Muhimma’s own theme and style instead of a style learned from reference decks.
  • Around it sit skills for the repetitive work: survey reports built straight from Excel (tables, heatmaps, charts, funnels), slide redesigns, covers and photos.

How they’re built

  1. 01

    Months of continuous training on real situations: scenarios, rules, corrections and direction from my own work.

  2. 02

    Full access to my knowledge vault: years of design rules, ideas and notes.

  3. 03

    A team of agents for research, design, images, icons, review and fixes, each checking the step before it.

    Almost zero mistakes by design: spelling, logos, brand elements and layout are checked before anything ships.
